# Business Requirements Document

## Problem
Sales stakeholders need a standardized view of revenue, target attainment, regional performance, product profitability and customer contribution.

## Users
Sales leadership, regional managers, business analysts and account managers.

## Core questions
1. Are we on target?
2. Which regions/products/customers drive growth?
3. Where are performance gaps?
4. What factors explain those gaps?
5. What action should management prioritize?

## Requirements
- Executive KPI dashboard
- Actual vs target
- Monthly and YoY trends
- Regional and product analysis
- Customer segmentation
- Root-cause drill-down
- Data-quality validation
- Exportable analytical tables
- Documented KPI definitions

## Success criteria
- 100% reconciliation of dashboard KPIs to validated source tables
- Zero duplicate transaction IDs after cleaning
- Documented metric definitions
- Recommendations supported by quantitative evidence
